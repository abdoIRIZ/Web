How to use?

Removing image backgrounds on Tindry is seamless and quick. First, drag and drop your photo into the dotted canvas area, or click the box to browse image files from your computer, smartphone, or tablet. The tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP image formats. As soon as your image is selected, our client-side processing engine analyzes edge contrast and isolates the primary foreground subject. You can instantly inspect the cutout over the checkerboard preview. Fine-tune your result using the Tolerance and Feather sliders to smooth delicate silhouettes or hair boundaries. Once you are pleased with the transparency quality, click the Download PNG button to save your clean cutout directly to your device with full alpha transparency preserved.

What is this tool?

Tindry Background Remover is an in-browser image extraction utility designed to strip backgrounds from digital photos and generate transparent PNG graphics. In modern e-commerce, digital marketing, and design, clean subject cutouts are necessary for Amazon product listings, Shopify catalogs, marketing flyers, social media collages, and corporate headshots. Instead of relying on expensive, heavy desktop graphic suites like Photoshop or waiting for slow cloud queues, this tool detects contrasting edges and separates subjects right inside your browser. The tool works across portraits, products, animals, and isolated graphic assets, delivering crisp borders and transparent alpha backgrounds without altering native image dimensions or degrading source resolutions.

Privacy & Security

Confidentiality and personal privacy are critical when handling personal portraits, unreleased product photos, or copyrighted artwork. Traditional image editors transmit your private photos to remote cloud servers where they may be stored, analyzed, or fed into machine learning datasets. Tindry protects your digital rights by executing 100% of the background removal process locally inside your browser private memory sandbox. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote host or external server. Nobody else can ever view or access your media. As soon as you navigate away or close your browser tab, all temporary image data is instantly erased, ensuring total data security.
