What is this tool?

Tindry's PNG to JPG converter is an instant browser-based graphic conversion utility that turns Portable Network Graphics (PNG) into universally compatible Joint Photographic Experts Group (JPG/JPEG) files. While PNG is ideal for transparent logos and vector graphics, it is an uncompressed lossless format that produces exceptionally heavy file sizes when used for digital photographs or complex illustrations. This causes sluggish web page load speeds, failed email deliveries, and mobile storage exhaustion. Furthermore, saving transparent PNGs on websites often results in jarring black borders. This tool solves both issues by compressing the image data efficiently and replacing transparent alpha pixels with a pristine white background layer, creating optimized files perfect for websites, blogs, and email attachments.

FAQ

Why does my transparent PNG turn black when converted to JPG?

JPG does not support alpha transparency. Without proper background rendering, transparent pixels appear black. Tindry solves this by automatically applying a crisp white background.

How much smaller will my file size be after converting from PNG to JPG?

For complex photos and detailed graphics, converting from PNG to JPG typically reduces file size by 60% to 80% without noticeable quality loss.

Will converting PNG to JPG blur fine text or lines?

At high quality settings (90%+), text and edges remain exceptionally sharp and clean for web and print use.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server during conversion?

Never. All conversion and color rendering occur strictly inside your browser using client-side Canvas APIs. Your files stay on your device.
