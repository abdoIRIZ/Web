What is Tindry's HEIC to JPG Converter?

The High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) is Apple's default photo format across iPhone and iPad devices running iOS 11 and later. While HEIC offers modern compression benefits, it frequently causes compatibility errors when attempting to view or upload photos on Windows PCs, Android devices, older browsers, and enterprise submission portals. Tindry's HEIC to JPG Converter decodes these Apple-specific image files and compiles standard, high-definition JPEG photos in seconds.

Why use Tindry's HEIC to JPG Converter? (100% Client-Side Privacy)

Smartphone photos contain private personal moments, family memories, and embedded EXIF metadata like GPS latitude and longitude coordinates. Cloud conversion sites require you to upload full-size camera rolls to third-party databases, presenting severe privacy risks. Tindry eliminates this vulnerability by executing 100% of the decoding and transcoding process within your local browser engine. Not a single byte is ever sent over the network.

How to convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

  1. Drag your .HEIC or .HEIF image from your phone or Mac into the upload area above.
  2. Tindry automatically initializes the browser's local WebAssembly decoder to read the raw HEIF container.
  3. Choose your preferred output quality (High 90% recommended for optimal balance between clarity and file size).
  4. Click Download JPG to immediately receive your universally compatible JPG image.

Why is Tindry free?

Traditional cloud services incur substantial computing costs converting heavy 48MP iPhone RAW photos on their servers. Tindry uses modern WebAssembly (Wasm) and HTML5 Canvas technology to distribute processing to your local device.
