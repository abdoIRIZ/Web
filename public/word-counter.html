What is Tindry's Word Counter & Case Converter?

Tindry Word Counter & Case Converter is a comprehensive real-time writing analytics and text formatting suite. Writers, editors, researchers, students, and software engineers frequently need to monitor word count constraints for essays, academic journal submissions, Twitter character limits, and SEO meta tags. In addition, our multi-format case converter instantly flips text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, and snake_case without requiring manual retyping.

Why use Tindry's Word Counter? (100% Client-Side Privacy)

When you paste confidential legal contracts, unreleased manuscripts, proprietary code comments, or private journal entries into online word counters, your text is routinely saved in server logs or used to train commercial language models. Tindry runs all tokenization, character counting, and string manipulation locally on your device's browser thread. No text is ever uploaded, cached, or seen by anyone.

How to count words and convert text cases?

  1. Paste or type your content into the main writing area above.
  2. Review your metrics: Total Words, Character Count, Sentence Count, and Estimated Reading Time.
  3. Click any transformation button (e.g. Title Case for headline capitalization or Clean Spaces to remove rogue tabs and trailing whitespaces).
  4. Click Copy Text to copy the converted result to your clipboard in one tap.
