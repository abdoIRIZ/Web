What is Tindry's QR Code Generator?

Quick Response (QR) codes are two-dimensional barcodes capable of storing URLs, credentials, plain text notes, and contact cards in a high-density black-and-white matrix. Unlike predatory QR generators that wrap your URLs in dynamic redirect links that expire after 14 days to force you into a monthly subscription, Tindry generates permanent, static QR codes directly containing your raw destination data. Once generated, your QR code will work forever.

Why use Tindry's QR Code Generator? (100% Client-Side Privacy)

When you create QR codes for private Wi-Fi network logins, personal telephone numbers, secret discount vouchers, or internal company links on traditional websites, your sensitive information is logged in remote databases. Tindry encodes the entire QR matrix inside your browser engine. Not a single character is sent over the internet, preventing data scraping and maintaining total confidentiality.

How to create custom QR codes?

  1. Select your content type: Website URL, Plain Text, Wi-Fi Network, Email, or Telephone.
  2. Type or paste your information into the input field.
  3. Customize the QR code styling by choosing custom foreground and background colors to match your brand palette.
  4. Select an output resolution: 512px for digital screens or 1024px for business cards and printed signage.
  5. Click Download QR Code to save a crisp, high-resolution PNG file to your computer.
