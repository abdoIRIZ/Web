What is Tindry's WEBP to JPG Converter?

WEBP is an internet image format pioneered by Google to provide superior lossy and lossless compression for web pages. However, when users save images from Chrome, Firefox, or Safari to their computers, they often find that image editors, Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and legacy operating systems cannot open or import .webp files. Tindry's WEBP to JPG Converter transforms WEBP files into universally accepted JPEG or PNG images in one instant click.

Why use Tindry's WEBP to JPG Converter? (100% Client-Side Privacy)

Conventional file conversion websites transmit your downloaded images to remote cloud servers to transcode them, retaining copies in server cache logs. Tindry performs every decode and encode calculation inside your local web browser sandbox using HTML5 Canvas. Your files are never transmitted across the network, ensuring complete confidentiality for screenshots, receipts, private photos, and proprietary graphics.

How to convert WEBP to JPG?
