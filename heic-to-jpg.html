What is this tool?

The High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) is Apple's standard photo format introduced across modern iPhone and iPad hardware running iOS 11 and later. Although HEIC provides excellent image compression and captures high dynamic range color, it frequently causes compatibility headaches when sharing photos with Windows PCs, Android smartphones, older web browsers, graphic editors, and official government or university submission portals. Tindry's HEIC to JPG converter bridges this gap by decoding proprietary HEIF file structures and transcoding the image into universally supported standard JPEG format. The converted images open seamlessly across all operating systems, media players, social media websites, and email clients without requiring third-party codecs or software installations.

FAQ

Why won't my Windows PC open iPhone HEIC photos?

Windows does not include native HEIC codecs by default, requiring paid extensions. Tindry converts HEIC to standard JPG so your photos open instantly on any PC.

Will converting HEIC to JPG reduce my photo's resolution?

No. Tindry preserves the full native pixel dimensions and aspect ratio of your iPhone photos without downscaling or blurring.

Can I convert HEIC photos directly on my iPhone or iPad?

Yes. Tindry runs inside Safari and Chrome on iOS and iPadOS, letting you convert HEIC photos to JPG right on your mobile device.

Are my photos uploaded to any remote server?

Never. All conversion operations take place strictly inside your browser using client-side WebAssembly. No files ever leave your device.
