How to use?

Generating custom, permanent QR codes with Tindry takes only seconds. First, enter or paste your desired destination content into the text field: this can be a website URL, Wi-Fi network credentials, plain text message, email address, or contact details. As you type, the live QR code matrix updates in real time on the preview canvas. Next, customize your visual styling: choose your foreground color (such as classic black, corporate navy, or custom brand hues) and select a contrasting background color. You can also pick from multiple export sizes ranging from 256px for digital screens up to 1024px Ultra HD for professional print banners. Finally, click the Download QR Code button to save your high-resolution PNG image directly to your disk.

What is this tool?

Tindry's QR Code Generator is an in-browser barcode creation utility that compiles raw text, links, and credentials into standard ISO-compliant two-dimensional Quick Response matrices. While many online QR generators trick users by wrapping URLs in temporary redirect services that expire or break after a trial period, Tindry creates genuine static QR codes. The encoded data is etched directly into the optical matrix itself, meaning the QR code functions perpetually without relying on external servers, third-party redirects, or internet dependencies. Whether you are creating QR codes for restaurant table menus, business cards, event posters, marketing packaging, or Wi-Fi logins, this tool produces clean, crisp vectors that scan reliably across all iOS and Android smartphone cameras.

Privacy & Security

Data privacy is critical when encoding sensitive information such as home Wi-Fi network passwords, private cryptocurrency wallet addresses, internal intranet links, or personal contact numbers. Traditional cloud QR generators transmit your entered strings to third-party servers, where your data and access logs can be recorded, cataloged, or monetized. Tindry protects your confidential data through 100% client-side execution. All QR matrix calculations and pixel drawing happen exclusively in your browser private memory. Not a single character of your input is ever sent over the internet or logged by external systems. Once you download your QR code and close the browser tab, all temporary data vanishes, guaranteeing absolute privacy and zero risk of tracking.
