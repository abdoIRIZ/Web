How to use?

Counting words and converting text capitalization on Tindry is instantaneous and simple. To get started, paste or type your text directly into the primary writing box above. As you type, the analytics dashboard updates live, displaying total word count, character count (both with and without spaces), sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time. To transform your text formatting, click any of the one-tap conversion buttons: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, or snake_case. You can also clean up formatting with 'Clean Spaces' to eliminate double spaces and irregular indentation. When you are satisfied with the formatted text, click the black Copy Text button to immediately copy the finished content directly to your clipboard.

What is this tool?

Tindry's Word Counter & Case Converter is a comprehensive client-side text analytics and formatting suite designed for writers, editors, students, researchers, social media managers, and software developers. Maintaining exact word and character limits is essential when drafting academic essays, college admissions statements, Twitter posts, Google Ads copy, and SEO meta descriptions. Whether you are reformatting article headlines, standardizing programming identifiers, or checking paragraph density, this utility processes your writing with zero lag and flawless typographic accuracy.

Privacy & Security

When you paste confidential legal contracts, unpublished book manuscripts, sensitive business correspondence, or personal journal entries into online word counters, your private writing is often uploaded to remote servers, logged in database records, or ingested to train commercial artificial intelligence models. Tindry guarantees complete confidentiality through a 100% client-side privacy model. All text tokenization, character counting, and case conversion routines run strictly inside your web browser's isolated local memory. Zero bytes of your written content are ever uploaded to any server or recorded anywhere. The moment you clear the text box or close the browser tab, all text data is permanently erased, providing absolute security and confidentiality for your private writing.

FAQ

How does Tindry calculate estimated reading time?

Reading time is calculated using the standard adult silent reading benchmark of 200 words per minute across your total word count.

Can I convert text into programming conventions like camelCase or snake_case?

Yes. Tindry provides dedicated buttons to format text into camelCase, snake_case, Title Case, UPPERCASE, and lowercase.

Is there any limit to the amount of text I can paste?

No. You can paste and analyze complete articles, research papers, or book chapters without arbitrary character caps.

Is my written content stored or saved on any server?

Never. All string calculations run locally in your web browser. Your text is never uploaded, recorded, or seen by anyone.
