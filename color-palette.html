How to use?

Extracting dominant color schemes from images with Tindry is straightforward and immediate. Begin by dropping your photo, artwork, or screenshot into the upload box, or click the area to select an image from your device. As soon as the file loads, our client-side quantization algorithm samples pixel clusters across your picture and identifies the five most prominent and harmonious color swatches. Each detected color swatch displays its exact hexadecimal (#RRGGBB) code alongside its RGB values. Click directly on any color card to copy its hex value instantly to your clipboard. You can also copy the complete CSS palette snippet with a single click or download a palette reference card to incorporate seamlessly into your design projects.

What is this tool?

Tindry's Color Palette Extractor is an in-browser colorimetric utility designed to analyze digital photographs, brand illustrations, and nature shots to extract dominant color harmonies. In web development, UI/UX design, digital marketing, and interior decoration, establishing a balanced color palette is fundamental to creating visually compelling experiences. Instead of manually inspecting individual pixels using an eye-dropper tool in heavy graphic software, this tool groups thousands of picture pixels into mathematical color clusters using fast k-means quantization. It calculates the primary dominant hues, secondary complementary tones, and ambient background shades automatically, outputting copy-pasteable Hex and RGB color specifications that can be integrated directly into Tailwind CSS configurations, Figma libraries, or graphic design mood boards.
