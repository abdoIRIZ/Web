How to use?

Capturing high-definition video of your screen with Tindry Record takes just seconds. First, customize your recording preferences below the preview frame: toggle 'Include Microphone Audio' if you want to record live voice commentary alongside your video. On Chromium browsers, you can also enable 'Stream Directly to Disk' for seamless long recording sessions. Next, click the black Start Recording button. Your browser will prompt you to select your capture source: choose your entire display, a specific application window, or an individual browser tab. Recording starts immediately with an on-screen timer. You can pause and resume at any moment. When finished, click Stop Recording, preview your capture, and click Download WebM to save your recording directly to your device.

What is this tool?

Tindry Record is a modern in-browser screen and audio capture utility powered by native browser MediaStream and MediaRecorder web technologies. Traditional screen recording software often requires bloated desktop software downloads, administrative permissions, or cloud services that impose restrictive duration caps and obnoxious watermarks. Tindry delivers a lightweight, browser-native alternative capable of capturing crystal-clear 1080p and 4K screen video with synchronized system audio and microphone narration. It is ideal for software engineers recording bug reproductions, teachers creating instructional tutorials, students capturing online webinars, remote workers archiving video conference presentations, and gamers streaming gameplay highlights. Because it runs directly inside modern web browsers, it operates smoothly across Windows, macOS, Linux, and Chromebook devices without installing any plugins.

Privacy & Security

Screen recordings routinely capture sensitive materials, including private email inboxes, confidential corporate presentations, financial reports, personal chat messages, and proprietary source code. Uploading these video captures to external cloud recording services introduces unacceptable security risks and privacy vulnerabilities. Tindry safeguards your sensitive data through a strict client-side privacy architecture. All video capture, audio mixing, and WebM encoding execute 100% locally within your web browser's isolated local sandbox. Not a single frame of your screen or packet of microphone audio is ever transmitted across the internet or stored on remote cloud servers. Once you save your file and close the tab, all temporary memory buffers are completely wiped, ensuring absolute privacy for your recordings.

FAQ

Does Tindry limit how long I can record my screen?

No. There are no artificial time limits. You can record short clips or hours-long meetings based on your device's available memory and disk space.

Can I record my microphone and screen audio together?

Yes. Check 'Include Microphone Audio' to mix your voice narration directly with the screen capture into one synchronized file.

Do I need to install an extension or desktop application?

No. Tindry Record utilizes standard Web APIs built into Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. No installation or setup is needed.

Are my recordings uploaded to any remote server?

Never. All capture and encoding happen locally on your device. Your video files never leave your computer.
